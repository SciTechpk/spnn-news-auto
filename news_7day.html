The investigating officer (IO) sought custody of the suspects for another five days for interrogation. However, Magistrate Wattoo granted the agency two more days of physical custody and directed the IO to produce the suspects again on Nov 5. According to NCCIA, the initial investigation revealed that the prime suspect operated a website to market and sell over 5m fake US postage stamps over a period of more than three years. According to the US Department of Justice, the website run by the Pakistani national was not authorised to sell USPS products and services. The counterfeit labels allegedly allowed customers to ship packages at rates “far below legitimate USPS prices”, it said, adding that it caused losses exceeding $126m to the postal service. US prosecutors alleged that the website sold the labels for around $2 each, regardless of a package’s weight, size or destination. A US court order authorised the seizure and shutdown of the website. Money trail In a press conference on Oct 20, NCCIA’s Lahore Zone Additional Director Atif Ameer said that 16 bank accounts had been used in the scam.
