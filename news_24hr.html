The International Cricket Council (ICC) said on Wednesday that it had penalised Pakistan’s Khurram Shahzad and Mohammad Abbas for breaching Level 1 of the ICC code of conduct during the first match of their ICC World Test Championship series against the West Indies in Tarouba. “Khurram was fined 15 per cent of his match fee and Abbas handed an official reprimand for breaching Article 2.5 of the ICC Code of Conduct for Players and Player Support Personnel, which relates to ‘using language, actions or gestures which disparage or which could provoke an aggressive reaction from a batter upon his/her dismissal during an International Match,’“ the ICC said in a press release. It added that one demerit point had also been added to the disciplinary records of each of the players, both of whom did not have any previous offences in a 24-month period. It also explained that Level 1 breaches carried a minimum penalty of an official reprimand, a maximum penalty of 50pc of a player’s match fee, and one or two demerit points. The ICC’s announcement of penalties came a day after Pakistan lost the Tarouba Test against West Indies — the first of a two-match series — by 90 runs on the fourth day of the Test.
