Laden with eggs, robots race up sacred Chinese mountain

A canine robot made by Beijing Lingyi Technology — a subsidiary of industry giant Unitree — completed the course in 43 minutes, 41 seconds, to win the four-legged speed category. Most tourists take at least two hours to complete the route, according to CCTV. The fastest humanoid robot — belonging to Beijing-based team Zhishen Lingyue — completed a shorter race course in 27 minutes and 26 seconds. Organisers called Mount Tai’s rocky terrain a “natural extreme testing ground” that could help train robots for real-world use, local media reported.
