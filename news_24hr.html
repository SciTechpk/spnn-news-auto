DIG Ali told the meeting that 18,560 personnel from the police and other law enforcement agencies wou­ld be deployed across Mir­pur Division for security. The deployment inclu­des 5,536 personnel in Mirpur district, 4,504 in Bh­imber and 8,520 in Kot­li. He said a three-tier security plan had been devised under which the police would serve as the first tier, civil armed forces as the second, and the Pakistan Army as the third. The second tier would comprise personnel from the Pakistan Rangers (Punjab), Pakistan Ran­gers (Sindh) and the Federal Constabulary, while 2,100 Pakistan Army troops would also be deployed to assist with election security. The DIG said all security forces would perform their duties under an integrated security plan on polling day, July 27.
