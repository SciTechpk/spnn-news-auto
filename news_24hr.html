WASHINGTON: The US has announced a new global visa restriction policy trageting foreign nationals involved in cybercrime and ​cyber-enabled crimes. The statement added the US would use a range of measures — including, sanctions, criminal prosecutions, asset seizures, extradition requests and enhanced international law enforcement cooperation — to dismantle criminal networks operating across borders. “By restricting visa issuance to those who are responsible for or complicit in these criminal enterprises, we are sending a clear message: the United States will go after those who prey on our citizens,” Rubio said.
